About EN PUC

EN PUC is a simple, student-friendly website designed to organise exam papers for 1st PUC and 2nd PUC students. It brings Model Exam Papers, Mid Term Exam Papers and Final Exam Papers together in one convenient place.

Website features

  • Separate sections for 1st PUC and 2nd PUC
  • Three exam-paper categories for each class
  • PDF previews for easy reading
  • A clean, mobile-friendly design
  • A custom EN PUC logo and welcome animation
Note: EN PUC is an independent study resource. Add only papers you are allowed to share, and check that each PDF is correct for the relevant class and syllabus.
